## November 24 disposition: historical comparison, current handover HOLD

The November 12 preference below is historical. The current source contract is [the November 17 protected 1S circuit](../hardware/2026-11-17-one-pet-protected-power-circuit.md) and [charging design](../hardware/charging-design-and-validation.md): ordinary BQ24075, one independently protected 1S pack with real NTC, a separately qualified 5 V buck-boost converter, and isolated dock contacts. BQ24074 is a comparison/reference only; do not purchase an Adafruit carrier, substitute another BQ variant, or add another charging owner from this historical note. No carrier or complete power assembly is qualified.

* TI [BQ24075 Rev N](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq24075.pdf) specifies OUT regulation at 5.4 to 5.6 V only with adequate input headroom. A nominal 5 V dock is not a 5.5 V regulated SYS source. With input absent and battery above 3 V, its specified BAT-to-OUT drop at 1 A is 50 mV typical and 100 mV maximum under the stated conditions. A 3.0 V cell design target is therefore not a guaranteed 3.0 V converter input. Measure actual minimum SYS at the converter pins, including pack PCM, connectors, wiring, transients and temperature; require derated converter operation at that minimum and margin above UVLO. Do not extrapolate the 1 A dropout specification to motor peaks.
* USB at the XIAO is a distinct service power source, not proof that the shared system or WROOM is powered. [Seeed's XIAO ESP32S3 guidance](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/xiao_esp32s3_getting_started/) requires isolation of an external supply at its USB-connected 5 V pin. Preserve the canonical series diode, verify its loaded forward drop and reverse isolation, and keep the onboard XIAO battery charging path separate from the BQ24075 pack. Verify the exact WROOM carrier's USB and regulator topology too; do not join USB VBUS to dock or shared actuator rails without reviewed source isolation. USB-only service must not backfeed the dock, motors or unpowered peripherals.
* Existing `size_5v_supply`, `screen_bq2407x_output_path`, `assess_docked_load` and `screen_one_cell_power_stack` in [power_preflight.py](../../code/scripts/power_preflight.py) distinguish the current and voltage arithmetic gates from physical qualification. They do not model switchover time, rail droop, cable losses, actual converter input during dropout, or MCU resets. Existing [tests](../../code/tests/test_power_preflight.py) cover variant/topology, independent current ratings and the explicit physical-false boundary. Do not relabel a paper pass as seamless transfer. The 3 V converter-input assumption needs re-evaluation against actual minimum SYS by the power owner before selecting hardware.

### Reset-free source-transfer acceptance (OPEN)

First use a qualified current-limited laboratory setup with no live lithium or energized exposed contacts; a battery emulator must be suitable for both source and sink operation before any charger transition test. Keep motors disarmed and preserve independent hardware cutoff. After schematic, protection, polarity and pack qualification, repeat using the actual protected pack under supervision.

Record simultaneous oscilloscope captures at charger IN, OUT/SYS, converter input/output, actual XIAO and WROOM local 3.3 V rails and EN/reset signals. Measure pack current with an explicitly declared polarity and independently calibrated measurement, plus USB/dock reverse current, converter and charger temperature. Use appropriate capture bandwidth and trigger on rail droop and contact changes; slow status polling is not transient evidence. Compare actual limits for each exact board and regulator, including [Espressif ESP32-S3 supply guidance](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-hardware-design-guidelines/en/latest/esp32s3/schematic-checklist.html), not just the brownout threshold.

Exercise dock insertion/removal, contact bounce and adapter dropout/recovery with the pack near its qualified low and high voltages, USB absent/present and USB insertion/removal while docked and undocked, and worst allowed simultaneous camera/WiFi/audio/display/servo hold load. Record both MCUs' boot/reset evidence and uninterrupted authenticated status/stream sequence; no reset or out-of-spec rail transient is acceptable in the qualified operating envelope. Any genuine loss of power outside that envelope must instead fail safely: independent motor cutoff, no automatic rearm after reboot or power restoration, and no falsely verified charging. CHG/PGOOD alone cannot prove net charge; battery supplement is possible even with dock input present.

All measurements, exact carrier revisions, waveform files, reset counters/reasons and repeat results are missing. Keep physical handover, dock charge and purchase HOLD. This is a source review and explicit acceptance boundary, not an electrical trial or firmware change.

TI product and datasheet: https://www.ti.com/product/BQ24074/part-details/BQ24074RGTR . Adafruit's published BQ24074 Eagle schematic and PCB are an actual open hardware implementation, not just a tutorial: https://github.com/adafruit/Adafruit-BQ24074-PCB . Its published license is Creative Commons Attribution ShareAlike with attribution requirements. Do not copy the board layout into this repo without recording the exact license text, version and required attribution. The retail board's intended USB/DC/solar use does not establish safe exposed robot dock contacts, pack protection, motor peak current or mechanical isolation.
2. **TI BQ25185**: active one cell charger with regulated 4.5 V SYS power path, selectable input current limit, thermistor protection and battery/system fault handling. Datasheet and product information: https://www.ti.com/product/BQ25185 . Its maximum 1 A charge rate and stated 3.125 A system discharge capability are distinct ratings. A charger IC is not a complete dock PCB, battery protection circuit or 5 V motor supply. No reviewed, pinned and compatible open hardware carrier design has yet been selected for this alternative.
3. **Existing PR #17 owner**: retain the existing read only MCP23017 seat/PGOOD/CHG observation, WROOM fail closed motion policy and Mac docking evidence. Neither software state nor AprilTag/IR agreement may substitute for independent charger protection, contact polarity and actual current/thermal measurements.

## Decision and incompatibilities

Prefer evaluating a purchased, documented BQ24074 power path board and its published CC BY SA schematic as a **bench reference**, not transplanting it into a finalized dock. BQ25185 remains a credible IC alternative, but changing to it would require a new qualified PCB and thermal/power budget. No firmware charge control, current threshold, BOM purchase approval or pin wiring changes follow from this comparison.

Both candidates are linear chargers: verify worst case thermal dissipation at actual adapter voltage, charge current, battery voltage and robot load. Characterize motor and servo startup peaks separately from average load; do not confuse charger current with available regulator output current. Require independently protected matched 1S cell, temperature sensing, fuse/current limiting, reverse polarity and short safe keyed contacts, power path isolation, hot removal and brownout tests. Document dock input and system rail transient behavior with a current limited bench supply before attaching any lithium pack.

## November 12 additional upstream comparison: BQ25895 is not a drop-in dock charger

TI's active BQ25895 is a switch-mode, I2C-controlled single-cell charger with NVDC system power path, a nominal maximum 5 A programmed charge current, and a separately advertised up-to-3.1 A OTG boost output. Source: https://www.ti.com/product/BQ25895 ; datasheet revision C: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25895.pdf . **Do not add 5 A and 3.1 A or assume they are simultaneous SYS and dock capacities.** The 3.1 A figure applies to OTG boost mode; SYS follows an NVDC battery-domain rail, not a guaranteed 5 V motor rail. The dock still needs a separately sized regulated 5 V load path, measured transients, protected cell, safe contact isolation and thermals. I2C configuration and watchdog recovery would add another failure mode relative to the existing read-only MCP23017 charger observation; WROOM motion safety must never depend on Mac charger control.

An existing public design discussion at https://github.com/brickbots/PiFinder/blob/release/docs/bq25895_design_notes.md is useful as a topology and integration checklist, **not** a verified reusable board or permission to copy its schematic. Its current license, commit pin, component revision and board design must be confirmed before any reuse. TI's datasheet may be linked and paraphrased; it is not an open hardware license. Adafruit's BQ24074 Eagle source is the only previously identified candidate with a published share-alike hardware design, but neither candidate has passed this robot's load or contact qualification.

**Research decision:** retain the existing charger candidate hold and the `power_preflight.py` sustained/peak rating gates. Do not add a BQ25895 driver, alter wiring, buy a charger, or energize the dock based on IC marketing current numbers. Next source-level work is to run the committed `test_power_preflight.py` on exact branch blobs; next physical work is to obtain a selected carrier's real input and SYS load curves and test with a current-limited bench supply before a lithium pack. This is a primary-datasheet comparison only; no source code, hardware or firmware test was executed for this note.
