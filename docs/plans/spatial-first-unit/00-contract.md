## Common spatial handoff

WROOM produces continuous `odom → base_link` in a single body boot/origin. Mac alone maintains `map → odom` from known-tag, stationary, fresh frames. Use +X forward, +Y left and +Z up. Keep `map`, `odom`, `base_link`, camera and tag frames explicit. Increment or invalidate the origin on pickup or reset. A stale transform cannot fill a new origin. Pose uses integer millimetres, milliradians and monotonic microseconds. Guide 20 owns the exact body wire contract. Guide 30 binds head-authenticated robot identity without equating clocks. Any consumer of a visual result binds the robot, camera boot, sequence, motion epoch and observation/session context before using it. [REP-105](https://www.ros.org/reps/rep-0105.html), [AprilTag upstream](https://github.com/AprilRobotics/apriltag)

The canonical body frame is right-handed: positive yaw (theta) is counterclockwise viewed from +Z, so a +90 degree rotation sends +X to +Y. Map and odom axes are fixed to their own origins; they do not rotate with the robot. A `PlanarTransform(parent, child, ...)` expresses the child in the parent; its point operation maps child coordinates into the parent. Compose map-from-odom with odom-from-base to obtain map-from-base. Do not negate a body pose yaw when decoding milliradians.

The reused dock camera calibration uses a different convention: optical X is right, optical Y down and optical Z forward; `mount_lateral_offset_mm` is positive to the right and `mount_yaw_deg` is positive pointing right. `FloorProjectionCalibration` converts these to base coordinates by negating the optical right component, mount lateral offset and mount yaw. Positive `pitch_down_deg` points below horizontal; it is not planar yaw. Image rows increase down, not along base +Y.

Grid cell coordinates are (x, y) along the fixed map axes, not image (row, column). `SpatialRuntime.localized_pose_cell_heading()` quantizes the map-relative yaw to a cardinal heading: 0 = +X (0 rad), 1 = +Y (+pi/2), 2 = -X (+/-pi), 3 = -Y (-pi/2). Left increments the heading modulo four; right decrements it. The planner uses the same four signed steps. This geometric convention grants no motion authority or physical qualification.

Digital demonstrations use recorded sensors, image fixtures, deterministic clocks and fake transports. Physical commissioning requires an identified assembly, settings, calibration, controlled procedure and actual measurements. Final first-unit release needs the existing A1–A12 evidence plus spatial repeatability from [guide 60](60-object-recall-and-navigation.md), verified mechanical and charge checks from [guide 70](70-fit-power-manipulation-and-dock.md) and the exact delivered cart from [guide 10](10-requirements-and-shopping.md). CI from a different SHA is historical. None of these guides records a passing physical trial or a checkout quote.
