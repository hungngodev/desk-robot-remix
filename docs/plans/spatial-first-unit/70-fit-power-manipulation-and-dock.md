# Printable assembly, protected power, manipulation, dock and station

Take [the shared contract](00-contract.md). Deliver a buildable robot, dock and read-only shared station package with verified fit and electrical interfaces, a print-at-100% set of 2D templates, and a safe commissioning order. Use the selected supplier variants from [10](10-requirements-and-shopping.md) and existing mechanical source. Guides 20 and 30 own MCU pin and serial implementation. Coordinate any changes in those contracts with their owners before assembly.

## Own these changes

Own `docs/cad/`, hardware fit/charge/power documentation and fixtures, dock-specific firmware/runtime, grasp fixtures/runtime and `code/firmware/room_station/` when those paths need revision. Agree `dock_runtime.py` and `grasp_feedback.py` boundaries with [90](90-integration-and-release-evidence.md) before shared registration. Guide 10 owns exact SKU commitments. Guide 50 owns camera and map conversion. Send it physical dimensions and tag calibration inputs.

## Work in this order

1. **Fit the exact part envelopes.** Use licensed SMARS originals and existing Fusion/OpenSCAD remixes. Measure selected carriers, case, cable exits, mounts, wheel track, floor clearance, battery restraint, servo sweep, speaker opening, grip inserts and docking contacts. Verify head PDM opening and fixed camera orientation through the complete lift/grip sweep. Export dimensioned 2D drilling, mounting and cut templates with a scale check and labeled front/back orientation, plus the corresponding printable solids and assembly view. Use paper overlays and individual fit gauges before committing to a full print. Include parts and fasteners by exact BOM role, assembly order and probe access.
2. **Validate power with loads isolated in stages.** Use the current protected 1S design as the integration source. Trace regulated logic and motor rails, pack protection, compatible temperature sensing, fuses/current limits, polarity, keyed connectors, local decoupling and independent cutoff. Check exact board revisions and safe voltage/current limits with 20/30. Verify continuity and polarity unpowered, then bring logic up on a current-limited supply before actuator, real pack or charge tests. Record idle, inrush, sustained and simultaneous loads, brownouts and regressions. Verify the independent cutoff under forced CPU stall using the chosen drivetrain. Establish coast distance and thermal envelope per drivetrain; qualify a winner before switching the purchase decision.
3. **Qualify safe desk travel and grasp.** Start tethered or on a catch fixture, from low-energy operation. Measure every safety lane’s sight line, range on actual surfaces, update and fault timing, stopping margin and swept turns. Exercise disconnections, stale/invalid data, bus failure and reset. Keep side/rear sensing read-only. Reuse existing servo calibration and grasp feedback, recording open and closed endpoints, force limits, joint interference, object geometry and burn-in. Demonstrate keys and wrapped candy with assistance recorded as a failure.
4. **Prove seat, charge and undock.** Reuse current passive guide, AprilTag alignment, terminal IR, contact readback and electrical evidence path. Confirm independent contacts, correct polarity, seating, pack temperature and actual net pack current under the supported test conditions. The verifier must distinguish tag seen, contact sensed and independently proven charge. Test wrong tag, missed or dirty contact, low supply and fault recovery before normal charging. Keep dock moves bounded through the body guard and reject blind recovery paths. Keep failures as explicit timed states and require physical review for changed dock geometry.
5. **Build the shared station independently.** Use existing Feather full profile and repository role list. Confirm each selected module’s bus mapping, voltage, enclosure, connector and data path. Expose only authenticated, timestamped read-only observations with a station ID, associated robot context and explicit stale state. Confirm multiple robots can read the same station without mixing personal memory or acquiring motion authority. Include a station-specific install and calibration page and the full required module list in the guide 10 handoff.
